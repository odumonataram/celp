About this platform

This platform was built as part of an MSc project on intelligent multilingual translation and indigenous language learning for the Communication in Niger Delta (CND) course at Federal University Otuoke. It supports English alongside four Niger Delta languages: Ijaw, Nembe, Epie, and Ogbia.

The dictionary, translator, and quiz content depend entirely on verified vocabulary supplied by the project's data collection -- the platform provides the structure; the linguistic accuracy comes from the fieldwork behind it.
